# Mita Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 22, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Mita ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Mita mobile application ("App") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our App.

By downloading and using Mita, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
We **do not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as:
- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Location data

### 2.2 Non-Personal Information
We collect only non-personal data necessary for app functionality:
- **Task Data:** Task titles and completion status (stored locally on your device)
- **Usage Data:** Simple completion analytics stored locally
- **Device Information:** Basic device type for ad optimization (handled by third parties, see Section 5)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Core Functionality
Your task data is used exclusively for:
- Displaying your daily tasks
- Tracking your completion progress
- Calculating streak statistics
- Persisting your data across app sessions

### 3.2 Local Storage
All task data and streak information is stored **only** on your device using:
- SQLite database (local storage)
- SharedPreferences (simple key-value storage)

We do not sync, transmit, or store your data on any external servers.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 No Third-Party Data Sharing
We do **not** share your personal or task data with any third parties.

### 4.2 Service Providers
We do not use any external service providers for data storage or processing.

## 5. Third-Party Services

### 5.1 Google AdMob
The App uses Google AdMob (by Google LLC) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:
- Device identifiers
- Ad interaction data
- Usage data

**AdMob Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 5.2 Google Mobile Ads SDK
Our App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving. This SDK may collect:
- Advertising ID (for ad personalization)
- Device type and operating system
- App usage data (for ad analytics)

You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting your advertising ID in your device settings.
