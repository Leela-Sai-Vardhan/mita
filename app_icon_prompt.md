# App Icon Design Prompt for Mita

Use this prompt to generate an app icon from an AI image generator (like Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, or similar).

---

## Prompt

```
Create a modern mobile app icon for "Mita" - a productivity/task management app.

## Brand Identity
- App Name: Mita
- Tagline: "Focus on what matters • Three tasks. One day."
- Brand Personality: Minimalist, neo-brutalist, focus-driven

## Visual Requirements

### Style
- Modern minimalist design
- Flat or semi-flat design (no 3D effects)
- Bold, high contrast
- Clean geometric shapes

### Colors (USE EXACTLY THESE)
- Background: Solid #0D0D0D (near black)
- Primary Accent: #E94560 (vibrant coral red/pink)
- Success Green: #4CAF50 (for perfect day indicator)
- Text/Icon: #FFFFFF (white)

### Symbol/Logo
Option A - Circle symbol: ⊙ (Unicode U+2299)
Option B - Abstract "M" letterform
Option C - Simple checkmark/target symbol

### Typography
- "Mita" text in bold, sans-serif font
- Use Space Grotesk or similar geometric sans

## Technical Requirements (CRITICAL - Google Play Store)

### Specifications
- Format: PNG (recommended) or JPEG
- Dimensions: 512 x 512 pixels
- Color space: sRGB
- Max file size: 1024KB (1MB)
- Bit depth: 32-bit (RGBA)

### Restrictions
- NO transparency - use solid background
- NO rounded corners in the design (Google Play adds them automatically)
- NO drop shadows extending beyond the icon boundary
- NO gradient edges
- NO professional photography
- NO device frames/screenshots
- NO text except app name

### Background
- Must be solid color (not transparent)
- Recommend: #0D0D0D or #1A1A1A (brand colors)

## Design Ideas

### Concept 1: Circle Symbol
- Solid black square background (#0D0D0D)
- White circle with coral red center dot (⊙)
- "Mita" text below or beside

### Concept 2: Minimal M
- Solid black background
- White bold "M" letterform in Space Grotesk style
- Coral red accent dot on uppercase M

### Concept 3: Checkmark Focus
- Solid black background
- Simple white checkmark icon
- Coral red circle background behind checkmark

## Deliverables
1. 512x512 PNG icon (primary)
2. Alternative versions (optional):
   - Light background variant
   - Different symbol options

## Important Notes
- Icon must be recognizable even at 48x48 pixels (Play Store minimum)
- Avoid fine details that disappear at small sizes
- Keep design simple and memorable
- Make it stand out among other productivity apps
